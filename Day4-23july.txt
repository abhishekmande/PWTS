2. Comparison Operator: checks between 2 values, if there are more than 2 also then also first 2 values are checked
	a) Equal to(==): checks wether 2 values are same or not
	
		eg. let var1 = 10
			let var2 = 10
			console.log(var1 == var2) //op: true

		eg. let var1=10
			let var2="10"
			console.log(var1==var2)//op: true
			//JS or TS will Implicitly convert var2 from string to number
			
		eg. let var1=10
			let var2="TS"
			console.log(var1==var2)//op: false
			
	b) Not Equal to(!=)
	c) Strict Equal to (===): check if the 2 values along with the datatype of variable are same or not
			eg. let var1=10
				let var2="10"
				console.log(var1===var2)//op: false
							
	d) Strict Not Equal to (!==): 
			eg. let var1=10
				let var2="10"
				console.log(var1!==var2)//op: true
				
	e) Greater than (>)
	f) greater than or equal to (>=)
	g) less than (<)
	h) less than or equal (<=)
